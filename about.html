About ASAGE’25

Advances in Sustainable Architecture and Green Engineering

About the Conference

ASAGE’25 is an international conference focused on the theme of “Advances in Sustainable Architecture and Green Engineering.” It aims to bring together researchers, academicians, and industry professionals to discuss innovative approaches and emerging technologies that drive sustainable development in civil and environmental engineering.

The conference provides a platform for interdisciplinary collaboration, encouraging participants to share their insights, findings, and best practices in green construction, smart materials, renewable energy applications, and eco-friendly infrastructure solutions.

Objectives

The primary objectives of ASAGE’25 are to:

  • Promote research and innovation in sustainable and green engineering technologies.
  • Encourage collaboration between academia, industry, and government institutions.
  • Highlight emerging trends in energy-efficient and environmentally responsible design.
  • Provide networking opportunities for young researchers and professionals.
