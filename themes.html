Conference Themes

The ASAGE’26 Conference invites research papers, case studies, and innovative ideas across the following broad themes of Structural and Geotechnical Engineering. The themes aim to bring together academics, researchers, and professionals to share insights into sustainable and technological advancements in civil engineering.

Structural Engineering

  • Advanced Concrete and Steel Structures
  • Earthquake and Wind Engineering
  • Bridge Design and Dynamics
  • Computational Mechanics and Structural Optimization
  • Smart Structures and Health Monitoring

Geotechnical Engineering

  • Ground Improvement Techniques
  • Foundation Design and Analysis
  • Geosynthetics and Reinforced Earth Structures
  • Seismic Response of Geotechnical Systems
  • Slope Stability and Landslide Mitigation

Materials and Sustainability

  • Innovative Construction Materials
  • Green Building Technologies
  • Durability and Service Life Assessment
  • Recycling and Reuse in Construction
  • Energy-Efficient Structural Systems

Emerging Technologies and Interdisciplinary Approaches

  • Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning in Civil Engineering
  • Building Information Modelling (BIM)
  • Smart Cities and Sustainable Infrastructure
  • Disaster Resilience and Risk Reduction
  • Automation and Robotics in Construction
